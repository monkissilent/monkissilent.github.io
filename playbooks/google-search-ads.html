Search Acquisition Deep-Dive

Grow qualified acquisition without wasting the budget.

The Search work provides the clearest detailed case study: a disciplined starting budget, structured intent discovery, progressive keyword control and continuous optimisation around registration performance.

S

Situation

MNF.ai, an AI-powered SaaS platform for filmmakers, needed a measurable search-acquisition engine capable of generating qualified registrations under a disciplined daily budget.

T

Task

Identify genuine filmmaker search intent, turn validated demand into efficient registration opportunities, and continuously improve performance as conversion data matured.

A

Action

Built a two-phase intent framework: first discover real search behaviour using structured Broad Match and Search Terms Reports; then extract validated winners into Exact and Phrase Match campaigns while reallocating budget and tuning bids.

R

Result

Managed ₹3L+ in aggregate Google Ads spend, delivered 30.8K+ targeted ad clicks, logged 14.1K+ total conversion signals and generated 10.5K+ website registrations.

The Strategic Framework

Discover first. Scale what proves itself.

The strategy separated exploration from efficiency, allowing the campaign to learn from real search behaviour before concentrating spend on validated demand.

PHASE 01 · INTENT DISCOVERY

Mine real search behaviour.

Understand what people actually searched for rather than assuming high-volume keywords automatically represented valuable demand.

  • Structured Broad Match keywords
  • Tightly themed ad-group structures
  • Daily Search Terms Report analysis
  • Aggressive negative-keyword additions
PHASE 02 · SCALE & OPTIMISE

Concentrate spend on validated intent.

Once winning keyword signals were identified, move them into more controlled performance buckets designed for efficient registration acquisition.

  • Exact and Phrase Match deployment
  • Broad Match retained as a discovery layer
  • Dynamic allocation toward top-performing assets
  • Optimisation against registration-cost trends
DISCOVER → VALIDATE → OPTIMISE → SCALE
Optimisation Loop

Turn search behaviour into campaign decisions.

Optimisation was treated as a recurring decision loop rather than a one-time setup. Query quality, relevance, conversion behaviour and cost signals informed the next action.

SEARCH TERMS → INTENT ANALYSIS → NEGATIVES → KEYWORD REFINEMENT → AD / LANDING RELEVANCE → CONVERSION DATA → BID + BUDGET DECISIONS

Intent-Match Hygiene

Conducted regular Search Terms reviews and added negative entries to reduce irrelevant traffic and budget leakage.

Creative & Landing Optimisation

Tested ad variants and aligned query intent with specific product messaging and landing-page experience.

Budget & Bid Optimisation

Used campaign performance signals to refine bids and concentrate spend where evidence supported stronger acquisition efficiency.

Bidding & Budget Management

A bidding lifecycle built around data maturity.

The campaign started with a controlled operating budget and evolved its bidding approach as stronger conversion signals accumulated.

Started at ₹500/day

Began with a disciplined daily budget and increased spend as performance validated additional scaling opportunities.

Start with learning

Initiated with Maximise Clicks to populate learning cohorts and understand search behaviour.

Move toward efficiency

Shifted bidding goals toward registration-cost optimisation as conversion data matured.

CONTROLLED START → LEARNING → CONVERSION SIGNALS → EFFICIENCY → SCALE

Decision Log

Optimisation decisions were driven by signals.

The recurring question was not simply “what should I change?” but “what does the evidence suggest, and what decision follows from it?”

Signal Decision Why
Search query quality Add negative keywords Reduce irrelevant traffic and protect budget.
Validated search terms Refine keyword / match-type structure Increase control around demonstrated intent.
Conversion data maturity Shift toward registration-cost optimisation Move from learning and traffic acquisition toward efficiency.
Campaign performance Reallocate budget and tune bids Concentrate spend where evidence supported stronger acquisition.
Ad / landing behaviour Test messaging and experience Improve relevance and reduce conversion friction.
