From Search Intent to 10.5K+ Registrations.

A two-phase, intent-driven Google Search strategy designed to maximise user acquisition efficiency within a disciplined ₹500/day operating budget. The framework separated search-intent discovery from high-efficiency registration scaling.

The Challenge

Grow qualified acquisition without wasting the budget.

MNF.ai needed to accelerate qualified filmmaker registrations while operating within a strict ₹500/day budget ceiling and simultaneously learning how its audience actually searched for the product.

S

Situation

MNF.ai, an AI-powered SaaS platform for filmmakers, needed a measurable search-acquisition engine capable of generating qualified registrations under a disciplined daily budget.

T

Task

Identify genuine filmmaker search intent, turn validated demand into efficient registration opportunities, and continuously improve performance as conversion data matured.

A

Action

Built a two-phase intent framework: first discover real search behaviour using structured Broad Match and Search Terms Reports; then extract validated winners into Exact and Phrase Match campaigns while dynamically reallocating budget and tuning bids.

R

Result

Managed ₹3L+ in aggregate spend, delivered 30,800+ targeted ad clicks, logged 14,100+ total conversion signals and generated 10.5K+ website registrations.

The Framework

Discover first. Scale what proves itself.

The strategy intentionally separated exploration from efficiency, allowing the campaign to learn from real search behaviour before concentrating budget on validated demand.

PHASE 01 · INTENT DISCOVERY

Mine real search behaviour.

The objective was to understand what people actually searched for, rather than assuming high-volume keywords automatically represented valuable demand.

  • Structured Broad Match keywords
  • Tightly themed ad-group structures
  • Daily Search Terms Report analysis
  • Aggressive negative-keyword additions
PHASE 02 · SCALE & OPTIMISE

Concentrate spend on validated intent.

Once winning keyword signals were identified, they were moved into high-performance buckets designed for more controlled registration acquisition.

  • Exact and Phrase Match deployment
  • Broad Match retained as a discovery layer
  • Dynamic allocation toward top-performing assets
  • Optimisation against registration-cost trends
DISCOVER → VALIDATE → OPTIMISE → SCALE
Bidding & Budget Management

A bidding lifecycle built around data maturity.

Bidding was continuously tuned as the campaign accumulated stronger signals, rather than treating the initial bidding configuration as permanent.

₹500/day allocation

Maintained a disciplined daily spending framework throughout execution.

Start with learning

Initiated with Maximise Clicks to populate learning cohorts rapidly.

Move toward efficiency

Transitioned bidding goals toward registration-cost optimisation as conversion data matured.

Core Optimisation Rules

Protect intent. Improve relevance. Remove friction.

Search-only networks

Avoided Search Partner and Display expansion networks to enforce stricter user-intent filtering.

Intent-match hygiene

Conducted aggressive weekly Search Terms reviews and added negative entries to prevent budget leakage.

Creative & landing tuning

Continuously tested ad variants and aligned query intent with specific product messaging.

Measurement & Growth Infrastructure

Paid media performance doesn't exist in isolation.

The acquisition framework was supported by behavioural analytics and conversion tracking so that campaign performance could be evaluated beyond clicks alone.

GA4 & GTM

Granular conversion-event triggers to monitor deep-funnel website signups and behaviours.

Microsoft Clarity

Session replays and heatmaps used to identify registration-layout friction.

PostHog & GSC

Behavioural cohorts and organic baseline signals used alongside paid acquisition data.
