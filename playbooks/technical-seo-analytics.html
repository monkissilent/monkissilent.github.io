The Challenge

Build a search-ready website through a major transition.

A website revamp and domain transition introduce technical SEO risks while also creating an opportunity to improve structure, intent alignment, user experience and measurement.

S

Situation

MNF.ai was evolving from its legacy web presence to a redesigned website and new domain, creating the need for a technically sound search foundation and a better user experience.

T

Task

Build a technically sound SEO foundation, address migration risks, align content with search intent, improve UX/CRO, and establish reliable analytics and conversion measurement.

A

Action

Used technical crawls, validated canonical paths and metadata, implemented schema and 301 redirect rules, mapped content to search intent, configured GSC/GA4/GTM, and used behavioural and conversion tracking.

R

Result

The revamped MNF.ai site established measurable search visibility and website engagement, with 75.7K+ search impressions, 1.78K+ search clicks, 149K+ pageviews and 65K+ active users in the available data.

Decision Framework

Diagnose the problem before choosing the optimisation.

Crawlability problem?

Fix technical architecture, robots.txt, redirects, canonicals and sitemap signals first.

Indexation problem?

Validate crawl paths, canonical signals, sitemap coverage and indexing behaviour.

Visibility problem?

Investigate search intent, content relevance, keyword opportunities and on-page structure.

Traffic but weak engagement?

Investigate landing-page experience, UX, behavioural signals and intent mismatch.

Traffic but weak conversion?

Move from acquisition diagnosis into CRO, tracking and conversion-signal analysis.

Measurement gap?

Validate GSC, GA4, GTM and behavioural tracking before making performance conclusions.
