Performance Acquisition Deep-Dive

Capture demand that doesn't begin with a search.

Unlike Search Ads, Meta reaches people who are not actively looking for an AI scriptwriting solution. The challenge was to translate latent needs into relevant messaging while operating within constrained startup budgets.

S

Situation

MNF.ai needed qualified registrations, workshop conversions and lead actions from audiences who were not necessarily searching for an AI scriptwriting solution.

T

Task

Build a disciplined Meta acquisition system capable of moving cold audiences from passive attention to landing-page engagement, workshop response and qualified lead actions within constrained daily budgets.

A

Action

Layered demographic, interest and behaviour audiences; deployed Reels, carousels, video demos and sponsored creator posts; optimised for Landing Page Views; improved mobile landing experiences; and used Meta Pixel and analytics signals for optimisation.

R

Result

Available records show 1.42M+ impressions, 1.20M+ reach, 28.5K+ deep link clicks and 9K+ Landing Page Views, alongside 790+ workshop registrations and 275+ qualified leads.

Acquisition Engine

Four layers. One full-funnel acquisition system.

The playbook connected audience strategy, creative formats, landing experience and optimisation feedback instead of treating Meta as a simple traffic channel.

01 · AUDIENCE TARGETING

Find the people behind the need.

Targeting focused on audiences likely to relate to filmmaking, writing and AI-enabled creation.

  • Demographic targeting
  • Interest-based targeting
  • Behaviour-based audience layering
  • Filmmakers, authors and AI enthusiasts
02 · CREATIVE FORMATS

Match the creative to the audience.

Multiple native formats were deployed to translate passive attention into a reason to engage.

  • Native carousels
  • Video script demonstrations
  • Sponsored creator posts
03 · CONVERSION FUNNEL

Clicks only matter when the page converts.

Mobile-first landing experiences reduced friction between ad engagement and meaningful action.

  • Fast mobile loading

  • Landing Page View optimisation
04 · OPTIMISATION LOOPS

Feed real behaviour back into the system.

Meta Pixel and analytics signals were used to sharpen targeting, traffic-quality and campaign decisions.

  • Meta Pixel feedback loops

TARGET → CREATIVE → LANDING PAGE → PIXEL FEEDBACK → OPTIMISE → SCALE
Funnel Performance

Optimise for the action that actually matters.

The campaign architecture moved beyond generic link clicks and aligned objectives with increasingly valuable downstream actions.

Attention → Click

Creative formats and audience layers were tested to turn passive exposure into relevant engagement.

Click → LPV

Landing Page View optimisation was used to move beyond simple link clicks and focus on users who reached the destination page.

LPV → Registration

Low-barrier workshop and event experiences created a practical next step for prospects moving deeper into the funnel.

Registration → Qualified Lead

Lead-generation campaigns supported progression from audience engagement toward qualified sales-lead actions.

Placement Feedback

Feeds, Reels and Stories were evaluated to identify stronger delivery and traffic-quality opportunities.

Optimisation Rules

What made the system performance-focused.

Objective Alignment

Campaign objectives were matched to the business action being pursued rather than optimising every campaign for generic traffic.

Mobile Landing Experience

Loading speed, viewport behaviour and form friction were audited because paid acquisition quality depends on what happens after the click.

Pixel & Placement Feedback

Pixel signals and placement performance were used as feedback for audience, creative and delivery decisions.

Measurement Layer

Measure the funnel beyond the ad platform.

The measurement stack connected campaign delivery with traffic quality and on-site behaviour.

Meta Ads & Pixel

Meta Ads Manager and Meta Pixel supported campaign delivery, audience signals and conversion measurement.

GTM & UTM Tracking

GTM and UTM parameters connected paid traffic to downstream analytics and helped assess traffic quality.

GA4

GA4 provided the broader website and acquisition context needed to evaluate what happened after the ad interaction.

Clarity & PostHog

Microsoft Clarity and PostHog supported analysis of user behaviour, interactions and potential mobile friction.

META ADS → PIXEL → GTM / UTM → GA4 → USER BEHAVIOUR → CAMPAIGN DECISION
Decision Log

Signals became decisions — not just reports.

The operating loop was built around reading campaign signals, deciding what to change, and using the next data point to validate the decision.

Audience response → Refine

When audience response indicated stronger relevance in specific layers, targeting was refined around those signals.

Creative performance → Rotate

Creative formats were varied and tested to identify stronger engagement patterns rather than relying on one format.

LPV quality → Optimise

Landing Page View optimisation was used when the priority was improving the quality of traffic reaching the destination.

Placement performance → Test

Placement performance across feeds, Reels and Stories informed delivery and testing decisions.

Pixel signals → Improve

Conversion and engagement signals were fed back into campaign decisions to improve downstream optimisation.
