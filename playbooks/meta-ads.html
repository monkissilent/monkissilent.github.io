The Challenge

Capture demand that doesn't begin with a search.

Unlike Search Ads, Meta reaches people who are not actively looking for scriptwriting tools. The challenge was to translate unconscious needs into immediate pain-point messaging while operating within restricted startup budgets of ₹100–₹500 per day.

S

Situation

MNF.ai needed qualified registrations, event-workshop conversions and community pipeline growth from audiences who were not necessarily searching for an AI scriptwriting solution.

T

Task

Build a disciplined Meta acquisition system capable of moving cold audiences from passive attention to landing-page engagement, workshop response and qualified lead actions within constrained daily budgets.

A

Action

Layered demographic, interest and behavior audiences; deployed Reels, native carousels, video demos and sponsored creator posts; optimized for Landing Page Views; improved mobile landing experiences; and fed engagement signals back through Meta Pixel.

R

Result

Managed 1.42M+ impressions and 1.20M+ unique reach, drove 28,500+ deep link clicks and 9,000+ quality Landing Page Views, while generating 792+ workshop responses and 277+ qualified sales leads.

The Engine

Four layers. One full-funnel acquisition system.

The playbook connected audience strategy, creative formats, landing experience and optimization feedback instead of treating Meta as a simple traffic channel.

01 · AUDIENCE TARGETING

Find the people behind the need.

Targeting focused on audiences likely to relate to filmmaking, writing and AI-enabled creation.

  • Demographic targeting
  • Interest-based targeting
  • Behavior-based audience layering
  • Filmmakers, authors and AI enthusiasts
02 · CREATIVE FORMATS

Match the creative to the audience.

Multiple native formats were deployed to translate passive attention into a reason to engage.

  • Dynamic Reels
  • Native carousels
  • Video script demonstrations
  • Sponsored creator posts
03 · CONVERSION FUNNEL

Clicks only matter when the page converts.

Mobile-first landing experiences reduced friction between ad engagement and meaningful action.

  • Fast mobile loading
  • CTAs near the top fold
  • Minimal registration fields
  • Landing Page View optimization
04 · OPTIMIZATION LOOPS

Feed real behavior back into the system.

Meta Pixel and analytics signals were used to continuously sharpen targeting and acquisition decisions.

  • Meta Pixel feedback loops
  • GA4 and GTM analysis
  • UTM-based traffic quality analysis
  • Placement testing across feeds, Reels and Stories
TARGET → CREATIVE → LANDING PAGE → PIXEL FEEDBACK → OPTIMISE → SCALE
Funnel Performance

Optimize for the action that actually matters.

The campaign architecture moved beyond generic link clicks and aligned objectives with increasingly valuable down-funnel actions.

Landing Page Views

Optimized for Landing Page Views over simple link clicks to isolate higher-intent readers and improve traffic quality.

Lead Optimization

Nurtured passive audiences with low-barrier interactive workshop registrations designed to move prospects deeper into the funnel.

Placement Testing

Automated distribution testing across feeds, Reels and Stories to identify stronger delivery and lower-CPM opportunities.

Campaign Rules

What made the system performance-focused.

Goal-Oriented Objectives

Every creative format was explicitly aligned with a down-funnel objective rather than generic vanity traffic.

Mobile Landing Optimization

Loading speed, viewport behavior and form friction were continually audited across iOS and Android experiences.

Continuous Pixel Analysis

Custom registration signals were fed back into Meta Pixel to sharpen interest segments and support programmatic scaling.

Marketing Analytics Stack

Measure the funnel beyond the ad platform.

Meta Ads & Pixel

Meta Ads Manager and Meta Pixel were integrated to define custom audiences, measure engagement and scale creative formats.

Traffic Analytics

GA4, GTM and custom UTM parameter sets were used to analyze down-funnel traffic quality.

User Experience

Microsoft Clarity and PostHog analyzed viewport interactions, scroll behavior and mobile friction.
