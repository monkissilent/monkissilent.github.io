The Challenge

Scale communication without sacrificing relevance or sender reputation.

The audience contained two fundamentally different groups: 13K+ registered users and 125K+ cold prospects. The objective was to build a reliable system that could nurture both groups without treating them like one generic list.

S

Situation

MNF.ai had a large combined registered-user and prospect audience that required different communication paths, while large-scale sending also introduced deliverability and sender-reputation risks.

T

Task

Build an email engine that could segment audiences, protect inbox placement, automate nurture journeys and generate measurable engagement from both active users and cold prospects.

A

Action

Decoupled registered users from cold prospects, hardened the sending domain with SPF, DKIM and DMARC, introduced automated warm-ups, cleaned risky addresses, used PAS-based copy and orchestrated nurture workflows.

R

Result

Sustained engagement across 13K+ registered users and 125K+ cold prospects, achieving a 64% average open rate and 12% average CTR while maintaining list hygiene and a bounce-rate target below 2%.

The Engine

Four layers. One reliable email system.

The framework coordinated audience intelligence, technical trust, automated journeys and performance measurement rather than treating email as a simple broadcast channel.

01 · AUDIENCE SEGMENTATION

Relevance over generic blasts.

Separate audience paths were created around user status and intent, allowing messages to match the recipient's relationship with MNF.ai.

  • 13K+ registered filmmakers
  • 125K+ cold prospect profiles
  • Feature, workshop and tutorial messaging for users
  • Problem-centric value pitches for cold leads
02 · DOMAIN SECURITY

Make inbox placement a foundation.

Technical authentication and sender-reputation controls were established before scaling cold campaigns.

  • SPF authentication
  • DKIM authentication
  • DMARC configuration
  • Ongoing sender reputation checks
03 · AUTOMATION WORKFLOWS

Move from campaigns to journeys.

Automated nurture sequences and cold-to-warm campaign engines sustained engagement while reducing dependence on manual sends.

  • Automated nurture sequences
  • Cold-to-warm campaign structures
  • Trigger-based routing
  • Google Sheets-synchronised lead flows
04 · PERFORMANCE METRICS

Measure engagement and system health.

Performance was evaluated through engagement metrics, conversion signals, sender reputation and list quality rather than open rates alone.

  • Open-rate monitoring
  • CTR monitoring
  • Conversion-event tracking
  • Sender reputation and list hygiene
SEGMENT → AUTHENTICATE → AUTOMATE → MEASURE → OPTIMISE
Deliverability & Copy

Protect the sender. Then earn the click.

Deliverability controls and message relevance worked together: a technically trusted sender still needs useful, audience-specific messaging to generate engagement.

List Hygiene

Lead lists were cleaned dynamically with Bouncify to remove risky addresses and keep bounces under 2%.

PAS Framework

Email copy followed the Problem–Agitate–Solution structure to frame audience pain points and present a relevant solution.

Spam Controls

Subject lines and email bodies were checked for spam-trigger terms before high-volume scheduling.

Audience Nurturing

Different audiences. Different reasons to open.

The messaging strategy was intentionally separated by audience status and need instead of relying on one universal campaign narrative.

Registered Users

Triggered communications around feature releases, workshops, tutorials and product adoption.

Cold Prospects

Problem-centric value messaging designed to explain how MNF.ai could help prospects with their script-planning needs.

Dynamic Relevance

Personalisation fields and narrative paths were aligned with filmmaker status and the recipient's position in the journey.

Marketing Automation Stack

Infrastructure built for repeatable execution.

Outreach Engines

Instantly AI and Snov.io supported scaled delivery, automated warm-ups and sender analytics.

SES & Delivery

Amazon SES was combined with Brevo and Mailchimp for bulk and transactional delivery, with Bouncify supporting list hygiene.

Orchestration

Kestra handled advanced trigger routing with Google Sheets used to keep lead flows synchronised.
