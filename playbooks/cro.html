Business Context & CRO Objectives

The problem wasn't only getting traffic.

MNF.ai acquired traffic through Google Ads, Meta Ads, Organic Search and social channels. The CRO challenge was to make the next step easier — from landing-page arrival through registration and onward activation.

01

Primary Conversion Journey

Google / Meta Ad → Landing Page → Account Registration → Free Trial Activation. Workshop traffic followed a separate Landing Page → Workshop Registration journey. Lead-generation campaigns followed a Landing Page → Lead Form → Qualified Lead journey.

02

Observed Friction

Initial UX was opinion-driven. Clarity, PostHog, GA4 and behavioural evidence revealed unnecessary steps, navigation friction, form hesitation, mobile issues and long flows.

03

CRO Objectives

Reduce friction, improve mobile behaviour, increase completed registrations, improve landing-page progression and make the conversion journey easier to understand.

04

Core Principle

A landing page is not successful because it gets a click. It is successful when the user can easily take the next meaningful action.

Diagnose

From opinion to evidence.

Behavioural and funnel data was used to understand where users hesitated, repeated actions, abandoned forms, struggled with navigation or failed to progress.

Signal Observed What It Suggested CRO Direction
Dead / repeated clicks Expected interaction was unclear. Improve interaction clarity and page hierarchy.
Navigation friction Users struggled to find the next step. Simplify navigation and reduce decision points.
Form abandonment The initial commitment was too high. Reduce fields and capture only essential information.
Excessive scrolling Important information or action was too far away. Rework above-the-fold content and information hierarchy.
Mobile friction The experience was not sufficiently mobile-first. Rework mobile layout and conversion flow.
Back-and-forth behaviour The path contained unnecessary steps. Simplify the conversion journey.
Registration → activation drop-off Account creation was not the final business outcome. Improve post-registration progression and automate activation.

Diagnostic inputs included Microsoft Clarity session recordings and heatmaps, dead and repeated clicks, scroll behaviour, GA4 funnel analysis, PostHog user journeys and funnel progression, and Google Ads / Meta conversion signals.

Turn behaviour into a repeatable optimisation system.

CRO was treated as a continuous loop: understand the business objective, diagnose friction, form a hypothesis, experiment, implement, measure and iterate.

Optimisation

Reducing friction across the user journey.

The optimisation work covered the experience around registration, landing pages, mobile behaviour, navigation, messaging and post-registration activation.

01 · Mobile-first experience

Improved mobile layout and prioritised the conversion path for smaller screens.

02 · Reduced registration fields

Initial registration was reduced to Name + Email. Phone was removed from the first interaction; profile enrichment could happen later through automated emails.

03 · Simplified navigation

Reduced navigation friction and unnecessary decision points around the conversion path.

04 · Improved flow & messaging

Refined page structure, copy and messaging so the next action was clearer.

05 · CTA optimisation

Adjusted CTA placement and presentation around user intent and the desired next action.

06 · Automated verification

Used CAPTCHA-based verification to reduce unnecessary manual friction in the registration process.

07 · Automatic trial activation

Free-trial activation became automatic after registration, reducing post-registration friction.

Page speed

Page-speed considerations were included as part of the broader mobile and landing-page optimisation work.

Workshop registration

Applied the same friction-reduction thinking to workshop registration, including reduced form fields.

CAPTURE MINIMUM INFORMATION → CREATE ACCOUNT → AUTOMATE ACTIVATION → ENRICH PROFILE LATER
Experimentation & Decision Log

Version 1 → experiments → Version 2.

Meta A/B tests and Google Ads experiments supported the broader CRO process. After multiple experiments and iterations, a new landing-page experience was developed and Version 2 became the primary Sign Up Page.

VERSION 1 → BEHAVIOURAL ANALYSIS → CRO HYPOTHESES → MULTIPLE EXPERIMENTS → VERSION 2 → PRIMARY EXPERIENCE

Measurement Impact

Conversion improvement through friction reduction.

The objective was not simply to increase clicks. It was to improve the probability that a qualified visitor could successfully progress through the conversion journey.
